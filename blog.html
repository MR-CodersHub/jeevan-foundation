Avadi Lake-Front Green Corridor

Completed on 14 October 2025 Paruthipattu Lake Perimeter, Avadi

Over 40 volunteers, along with local school teachers, planted 120 native saplings along the lake bund to mitigate soil erosion and create natural pedestrian shade.

AUTHENTIC EXPERIENCES

Voices from Our Community.

“Before attending the Career Guidance workshop in Avadi, I was completely overwhelmed by engineering admissions. The mentors sat with me for two hours, reviewed my marks, and showed me diploma-to-degree lateral entry paths I never knew existed. Today I am in my second year with a clear direction.”

Selvi Murugan

Engineering Student & Mentee · Avadi

“Volunteering with Broken Plates changed my relationship with my own city. Handing over a hot, wholesome meal to an elderly day laborer and being greeted with so much grace taught me that true community work is about dignity, not charity.”

Dinesh Kumar

Volunteer, Broken Plates Initiative · 2 Years

“Our school boundary used to be dry and dusty. When Jeevan Foundation brought Seeds of Hope to our campus, they didn’t just plant trees—they assigned three students to each sapling with name tags. Watching our children water their trees each morning has built a deep love for nature.”

Mrs. Lakshmi Sridhar

School Headmistress · Chennai Suburban District
