OUR STORY

Change begins
close to home.

Jeevan Foundation is based in Avadi, Chennai, and focuses on practical community development. Through Seeds of Hope, Career Guidance and Broken Plates, the foundation brings attention and action to environmental care, informed career choices and food insecurity.

Our approach is simple: listen to community needs, take thoughtful action, and document our work honestly so people can understand what has been done and what still needs attention.
